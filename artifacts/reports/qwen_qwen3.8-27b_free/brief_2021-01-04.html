What changed

WarningTraffic up, revenue down. Sessions rose 42% to 29,029, but revenue fell 9.3% to 9,402 and revenue per session fell from 0.51 to 0.32.

WarningCheckout step weakened. Checkout -> purchase fell from 52.87 to 46.96, a -11.2% change, and total conversion rate fell from 0.68 to 0.56.

WarningUS AOV fell sharply. United States AOV fell from 79.96 to 47.32, -40.8%, with a -28.8 point gap versus total AOV.

PositiveMobile revenue recovered. Mobile revenue rose 62% to 3,385 and mobile AOV rose from 28.63 to 54.6, a +102.7 point gap versus total AOV.

Likely drivers

  • The previous week included New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, so the revenue and AOV decline may be calendar timing.
  • The total traffic_vs_revenue anomaly is consistent with sessions up 42% while revenue fell 9.3%.
  • The checkout-to-purchase step fell from 52.87 to 46.96, consistent with a checkout-stage issue.
  • United States AOV fell from 79.96 to 47.32, consistent with lower US order values.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the checkout-to-purchase decline persists, higher sessions will not convert into revenue.
  • If US AOV weakness persists, total revenue will remain below the prior week.
  • If source attribution is not validated, channel decisions may rely on unreliable splits.
