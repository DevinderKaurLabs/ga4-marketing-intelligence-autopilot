What changed

PositiveTotal revenue and transactions increased. Revenue was 59721, up 38.8% from 43042; transactions were 865, up 34.3% from 644; users were 31498, up 28.4% from 24534.

PositiveIndia outperformed the total. India revenue was 7512, up 117.9% from 3448, with gap_vs_total_pts of 79.1; India revenue per session was 2.06, up 72.1% from 1.2, with gap_vs_total_pts of 60.7.

WarningCanada lagged the total. Canada revenue was 4323, down 5.2% from 4561, while sessions were up 20.3%; Canada revenue per session was 1.47, down 21.2% from 1.87, with gap_vs_total_pts of -32.6.

WatchCheckout improved but product-to-cart remains the biggest leak. Product view to add to cart was 33.04, down 1.8% from 33.65; add to cart to checkout was 58.25, up 7.5%; checkout to purchase was 48.8, up 17.4%.

Likely drivers

  • No calendar event is listed for this week; the previous week included Cyber Monday, so the increase is likely not a listed-event spike.
  • India revenue and revenue per session moved far above the total, consistent with a country-specific demand increase.
  • Canada sessions rose 20.3% but revenue fell 5.2%, consistent with weaker monetization in that segment.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If Canada revenue per session stays at 1.47 while the total is 1.54, Canada could continue to drag total growth.
  • If the product view to add to cart step stays at 33.04, additional traffic may not convert into carts.
  • Source and campaign data quality issues could lead to decisions based on incomplete channel attribution.
