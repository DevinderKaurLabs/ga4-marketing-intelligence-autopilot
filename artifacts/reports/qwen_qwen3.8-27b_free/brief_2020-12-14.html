What changed

CriticalTotal revenue and transactions declined. Revenue was 47276 vs 59721, -20.8%; transactions were 685 vs 865, -20.8%.

WarningCart-to-checkout step weakened. Add to cart -> checkout was 48.49 vs 58.25, -16.7%, and is the biggest funnel leak.

PositiveDirect segment moved differently. Direct transactions were 65 vs 49, +32.7%, with gap_vs_total_pts 53.5; direct revenue per session was 0.86 vs 0.67, +28.9%, with gap_vs_total_pts 37.2.

WarningHigh-interest products did not convert. Android Large Trace Journal Black had 3401 views, 1420 adds to cart, and 0 purchase lines; Google F/C Long Sleeve Tee Ash had 6353 views, 2325 adds to cart, and 0 purchase lines.

Likely drivers

  • The total decline is likely consistent with fewer sessions and users: sessions 33411 vs 38729, -13.7%, and users 27421 vs 31498, -12.9%.
  • The conversion drop is likely consistent with the Add to cart -> checkout leak: 48.49 vs 58.25, -16.7%.
  • Direct traffic is likely a partial offset: direct transactions 65 vs 49, +32.7%, and direct revenue per session 0.86 vs 0.67, +28.9%.
  • Product performance may be contributing: Google Men's Tech Fleece Grey had revenue_delta -1226, and several high-interest products had 0 purchase lines.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the Add to cart -> checkout leak persists, users who add items may not complete purchases.
  • If high-interest products remain unconverted, product interest will not become revenue.
  • If returning purchasers continue to decline, revenue will rely more on new purchasers: returning_purchasers were 34 vs 57, -40.4%, and revenue_from_returning was 2514 vs 5877, -57.2%.
