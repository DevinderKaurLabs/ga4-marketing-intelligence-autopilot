What changed

PositiveTotal revenue and transactions rose sharply. Revenue was 38086, up 60.7% from 23694; transactions were 563, up 53.8% from 366.

WarningConversion rate declined. Conversion rate was 1.44, down 4.7% from 1.52, while sessions rose 15.1% to 25914.

PositiveUnited States outperformed total. US revenue was 17492, up 86.5% from 9381, with gap_vs_total_pts of 25.7.

Likely drivers

  • The total revenue lift is likely concentrated in the United States, which had revenue of 17492, up 86.5%, and a gap_vs_total_pts of 25.7.
  • Higher revenue per session, 1.47 versus 1.05, is consistent with more value per visit rather than only more sessions.
  • Mobile transactions grew 75.5% to 258, which may have contributed to the total transaction increase.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the conversion-rate decline continues, future session growth may not translate into revenue.
  • If the US lift is not verified, channel decisions may rely on partially obfuscated source data.
  • High-interest products with zero purchases, such as Google Blue Stojo Cup with 1467 views and 0 purchase lines, may indicate a checkout or product-page issue.
