What changed

PositiveTotal revenue and transactions rose. Revenue was 26966 versus 13150, a 105.1% change; transactions were 400 versus 253, a 58.1% change; sessions were 27478 versus 27707, a -0.8% change.

WarningProduct view to add to cart weakened. Product view -> add to cart was 18.63 versus 22.45, a -17% change and -3.82 points; the payload lists it as the biggest leak.

WatchDirect traffic and revenue fell. Direct campaign users were 3596 versus 5040, a -28.7% change; direct campaign sessions were 3724 versus 5173, a -28% change; direct campaign revenue was 1033 with a -38.6% change.

PositiveReferral campaign performance improved. Referral campaign revenue was 16252 with a 175.6% change; transactions were 246 with a 110.3% change; conversion rate was 3 with an 87.8% change.

Likely drivers

  • The revenue increase is likely consistent with stronger referral campaign performance, because referral campaign revenue was 16252 and changed 175.6%.
  • The revenue increase is likely consistent with a higher cart to checkout rate, which rose from 42.15 to 56.62, a 34.3% change.
  • The direct traffic decline is likely consistent with direct campaign sessions falling from 5173 to 3724, a -28% change.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the conversion rate and revenue per session spikes are tracking errors, the team may make decisions on inflated performance.
  • If product view -> add to cart remains the biggest leak, product interest may not become carts.
  • If direct traffic continues to fall, the sample may become more dependent on referral traffic.
