What changed

CriticalTotal revenue and conversion fell after the holiday week. Revenue was 10368 vs 14716 (-29.5%), transactions were 165 vs 257 (-35.8%), and conversion rate was 0.68 vs 1.06 (-36%).

CriticalMobile monetization fell more than the total. Mobile revenue per session was 0.26 vs 0.59 (-55.4%, gap_vs_total_pts -31.5) and mobile AOV was 28.63 vs 44.85 (-36.2%, gap_vs_total_pts -45.9).

CriticalCanada fell more than the total. Canada revenue per session was 0.29 vs 1.34 (-78.4%, gap_vs_total_pts -54.5) and Canada conversion rate was 0.51 vs 1.54 (-67.1%, gap_vs_total_pts -31.1).

PositiveAOV rose while transactions fell. Total AOV was 62.84 vs 57.26 (+9.7%) and desktop AOV was 92.42 vs 68.74 (+34.4%, gap_vs_total_pts 24.7), while total transactions were 165 vs 257 (-35.8%).

WarningHigh-interest products had zero purchases. Android Iconic Crewneck Sweatshirt had views_cur 1366, adds_to_cart_cur 486, purchase_lines_cur 0, view_to_purchase_pct 0, and revenue_delta -616.

Likely drivers

  • The week followed Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day, so the drop is likely a post-holiday demand reset.
  • New Year's Eve and New Year's Day may have reduced purchase intent, consistent with lower transactions and conversion.
  • Mobile and Canada gaps are consistent with a larger post-holiday reset in those segments, but the size of the gaps suggests segment-specific issues to verify.
  • The AOV increase is consistent with fewer, larger orders after the holiday peak.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If mobile and Canada issues are not checked, the revenue decline may continue into the next week.
  • If high-interest products remain at zero purchases, product demand will not convert.
  • If source data is not treated with caution, channel decisions may be based on obfuscated or missing source values.
