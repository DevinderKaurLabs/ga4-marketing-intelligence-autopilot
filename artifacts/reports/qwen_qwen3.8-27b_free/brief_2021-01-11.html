Likely drivers

  • No calendar events are provided, so the lift is not explained by a known seasonal event.
  • The revenue lift is consistent with conversion rate rising from 0.56 to 0.82 and revenue per session rising from 0.32 to 0.47.
  • The improvement is likely supported by the add-to-cart to checkout step rising from 35.94 to 42.15.
  • New-purchaser revenue rose from 8321 to 12041, while returning-purchaser revenue was 1147, so the week likely relied more on new buyers.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If sessions continue to fall, the conversion lift may not offset the traffic decline.
  • AOV fell to 51.98, so revenue per transaction may weaken if basket value does not recover.
  • Source attribution is incomplete, with 24.4% of session source rows unusable, so channel performance may be misread.
  • High-interest products with zero purchase lines may leave demand unconverted.
