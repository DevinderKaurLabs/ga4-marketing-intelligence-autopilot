What changed

PositiveTotal revenue up. Revenue was 23,694, up 8.6% from 21,820; transactions were 366, up 25.8% from 291; sessions were 22,508, down 9.9% from 24,991.

WarningConversion-rate increase needs verification. Conversion rate was 1.52, up 52.1% from 1.0; the payload marks this figure with check_tracking true.

PositiveReferral revenue outperformed total. Referral campaign revenue was 13,378, up 26.2% from 10,597, with gap_vs_total_pts 17.7 and segment_share_pct 56.5.

PositiveMobile revenue outperformed total. Mobile revenue was 10,610, up 35.3% from 7,842, with gap_vs_total_pts 26.7; mobile revenue per session was 1.19, up 51.6% from 0.78.

CriticalCanada revenue underperformed total. Canada revenue was 1,739, down 45.3% from 3,181, with gap_vs_total_pts -53.9; Canada AOV was 57.97, down 41.7% from 99.41, with gap_vs_total_pts -28.

Likely drivers

  • No calendar events are present, so seasonality cannot be explained from the payload.
  • The total revenue increase is consistent with referral campaign revenue of 13,378, up 26.2%, and mobile revenue of 10,610, up 35.3%.
  • The Canada decline is likely a segment-specific issue because Canada revenue was 1,739, down 45.3%, while total revenue was up 8.6%.
  • The direct-traffic weakness is consistent with direct revenue per session of 0.23, down 41.9%, and direct checkout-to-purchase rate of 14.53, down 32.2%.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the conversion-rate figure is not verified, the team may act on a tracking artifact instead of real demand.
  • If Canada and direct underperformance are ignored, the business may remain exposed to segment declines.
  • If source data quality is ignored, channel actions may be based on 24.4% unknown or obfuscated source rows.
