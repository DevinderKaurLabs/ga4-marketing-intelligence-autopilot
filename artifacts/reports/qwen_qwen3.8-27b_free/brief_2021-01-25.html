What changed

CriticalTotal revenue and order value fell sharply. Revenue was 5178 vs 26966 (-80.8%), AOV was 15.32 vs 67.42 (-77.3%), and revenue per session was 0.2 vs 0.98 (-80%).

WarningTraffic was stable but conversion weakened. Sessions were 26342 vs 27478 (-4.1%), users were 21590 vs 22647 (-4.7%), and conversion rate was 1.18 vs 1.36 (-13.8%).

WarningCheckout step weakened. Add to cart -> checkout was 53.31 vs 56.62 (-5.9%, -3.31 points) and was the biggest funnel leak.

WarningSome countries showed weaker product view to cart. Canada view_to_cart_rate was 16.85 vs 22.11 (-23.8%) and India was 13.29 vs 16.91 (-21.4%).

PositiveOrganic traffic increased. Organic sessions were 13640 vs 12036 (+13.3%) and organic users were 12644 vs 11351 (+11.4%).

Likely drivers

  • No calendar events are provided, so seasonality cannot be explained from the payload.
  • The total revenue drop is likely consistent with the large drop in AOV (15.32 vs 67.42) and revenue per session (0.2 vs 0.98), while sessions and users changed less.
  • The funnel change is consistent with a weaker Add to cart -> checkout step (53.31 vs 56.62).
  • The revenue drop is consistent with lower revenue on high-revenue items, including Google Crewneck Sweatshirt Green revenue of 275 vs 1925 and Gift Card- $100.00 revenue of 0 vs 1200.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the AOV and revenue per session drop is not verified, marketing may act on a tracking error.
  • If the checkout leak is not addressed, more carts may stop before purchase.
  • If high-value at-risk customers are not retained, the at-risk high value segment may keep growing from 470 to 668.
