Week of 2020-11-23 to 2020-11-29
Compared with the week of 2020-11-16
US Thanksgiving, Black Friday

Revenue rose 26% to 47973 in the Thanksgiving/Black Friday week, but the add-to-cart to checkout step fell to 102.82 from 196.99.

Summary

  • Total revenue was 47973, up 26% from 38086, with transactions at 681, up 21% from 563.
  • Sessions were 27780, up 7.2%, and users were 22126, up 10.8%, consistent with the US Thanksgiving and Black Friday calendar.
  • Conversion rate was 1.85, up 28.5% from 1.44, and AOV was 70.44, up 4.1% from 67.65.
  • The biggest funnel leak was Add to cart -> checkout, which moved to 102.82 from 196.99, a -47.8% change.

What changed

PositiveTotal revenue and transactions increased. Revenue was 47973 vs 38086, +26%; transactions were 681 vs 563, +21%; sessions were 27780 vs 25914, +7.2%.

CriticalAdd to cart -> checkout declined. Add to cart -> checkout was 102.82 vs 196.99, -47.8%, and is the biggest leak.

PositiveReferral channel converted strongly. shop.googlemerchandisestore.com / referral revenue was 13458, +29.4%, with conversion rate 2.97, +62.7%, while sessions were 4949, +0.4%.

WatchMobile view-to-cart moved below total. Mobile product view to cart rate was 18.64 vs 8.42, +121.5%, with gap_vs_total_pts -16.8; total view-to-cart is flagged for tracking verification.

Likely drivers

  • The week included US Thanksgiving and Black Friday, likely driving higher sessions, users, and revenue.
  • New users were 19299, up 12.9% from 17100, consistent with holiday shopping traffic.
  • The add-to-cart to checkout decline is not explained by the calendar events in the payload and likely needs a checkout or tracking investigation.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the add-to-cart to checkout step remains at 102.82 vs 196.99, checkout completions may continue to be lost.
  • If the product view to cart rate is not verified, teams may act on a tracking artifact instead of a demand change.
  • If new purchasers are not followed up, returning revenue share may remain low at 2.21 vs 3.28.
