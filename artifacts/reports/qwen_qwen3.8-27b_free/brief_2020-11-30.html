Likely drivers

  • The previous week included US Thanksgiving and Black Friday, while this week included Cyber Monday, so the revenue decline is likely consistent with post-Black Friday seasonality.
  • The funnel shows the main change in Add to cart -> checkout, so the revenue decline is likely consistent with a checkout-step issue rather than a traffic issue.
  • Organic revenue per session moved more negatively than total, so the organic mix is likely consistent with lower revenue per session.

Risks if nobody acts

  • If the Add to cart -> checkout leak is not addressed, the total anomaly has segment_revenue_delta -4931.
  • If product view to cart tracking is not verified, actions may be based on a figure that is not a demand story.
  • If organic revenue per session is not reviewed, the gap_vs_total_pts of -16.7 remains a watch item.
